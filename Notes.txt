Step 1 — You fill a form (/plan page)
You tell the app your age, weight, height, how active you are, and what you want — lose weight, gain muscle, or stay the same. You also pick your diet type (veg, non-veg, keto, etc.).

Step 2 — The app does math (lib/calculations.ts)
It calculates your BMR (how many calories your body burns just lying in bed), then multiplies it by your activity level to get TDEE (total calories you actually burn per day). Then it adjusts up or down based on your goal. This gives your daily calorie + protein/carbs/fat targets.

Step 3 — AI builds your meal plan (lib/ai-meal-plan.ts → Gemini)
Your profile gets sent to Google's Gemini AI. It acts like a nutritionist and picks 5 Indian meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, 2 snacks) that fit your calorie target and diet type. Then it cross-checks the nutrition numbers against the USDA food database, and fetches a photo from Unsplash for each meal.

Step 4 — Meals get saved to the database (/api/meal-plan → Supabase)
Each AI-generated food item gets saved into the Food table in your PostgreSQL database on Supabase. This means next time the same food appears, it doesn't need to call AI again.

Step 5 — You see the results (/results page)
The results page shows your macro targets, a donut chart, and all 5 meals with photos, calories, and macros. Meals generated by AI get a ✦ AI badge.

Step 6 — You save the plan (Save button → /api/plans)
When you click Save, two things happen: your profile + macros go into the Plan table, AND the exact food items (with their DB IDs) go into the new PlanMeal join table. This is the link between your plan and the actual meals.

Step 7 — Dashboard shows saved plans (/dashboard)
Your dashboard lists all saved plans. When you click "View Plan", it passes the planId to the results page. The results page sees the planId, calls /api/meal-plan?planId=..., which reads directly from PlanMeal → Food in the DB — zero AI calls, instant load.

The database structure (simplified):

User → has many → Plans
Plan → has many → PlanMeals (the join table)
PlanMeal → points to one → Food item  (with mealTime + aiGenerated flag)
Food → stores all food data (from AI or USDA sync)
